Agent genome

What each agent knows

Almost nothing. Every speck has a position, a heading, and three "nostrils" pointing ahead, ahead-left and ahead-right at sensor distance. Each tick it sniffs the chemical trail at those three points, turns toward the strongest, steps forward, and leaves a little trail behind.

The dish then blurs the trail by one cell and multiplies it by the decay factor. That is the entire rule set.

What I found playing with it

The sensor angle to turn angle ratio is the steering wheel. Around 1:2 you get branching networks. Push the sensors wide and turning narrow and the mould collapses into drifting cells. Long sensor reach with slow decay gives fat, looping arteries.

Oats emit a constant chemical, so the network pulls itself into roughly the shortest tubes linking them, and prunes redundant branches as the weaker trails evaporate.

Why this is famous

In 2010, Tero and colleagues laid oat flakes in the positions of cities around Tokyo and let real Physarum grow between them. The tubes it built closely matched the efficiency and fault tolerance of the actual rail network, without any engineer involved. Try the Scatter oats button and watch the same kind of compromise form.
